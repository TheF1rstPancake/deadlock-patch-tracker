As Halloween draws near, the residents of The Cursed Apple have put out some decorations to celebrate their favorite holiday. The Hideout gains a spooky new look, our heroes don masks, and NYC is now littered with pumpkins and curious [i]Halloween Cauldrons[/i] that grant willing trick-or-treaters delicious candy.

[/p][h3][b]New this year - Halloween Cauldrons:[/b][/h3][p][img src="{STEAM_CLAN_LOC_IMAGE}/45164767/74c75f698993f653082fa1bfed91b2f9f5ce25d4.png"][/img]As you walk down the streets of Manhattan during a match, you'll come across bubbling cauldrons boasting a tempting display of treats. With a [i]Heavy Melee[/i], break the cauldron to pick up a piece of Candy and stash it in your pocket for later. When you're feeling a bit snacky, you can consume the candy to grant yourself a boost of energy with the temporary [i]Sugar Rush[/i] buff. - Who said candy is bad for you?

The Halloween festivities are available in-game now through November 2nd, 2026. Happy Haunting!

[b]General:[/b]
- Mid-Boss: Adjusted the terrain layout where the midboss is, the area is a little bigger now and has more cover

- Comeback gold from non-hero sources increased from a max of 26% to 30% (when behind by ~20% Net Worth)
- The bonus NPC souls multiplier when behind now also increases tick souls for the 2 poorest players

- Healing Snacks heal increased from 10% Max HP to 14%
- Steam Vents now increase stamina regeneration by 150%

- Move slow to air drag ratio increased from 0.35 to 0.5

- Move speed in tunnels reduced from +70% to +50%

- Latest 6 heroes are no longer categorized as "new" anymore. This means they have normal hero unlocking requirements for ranked and can now be banned.
