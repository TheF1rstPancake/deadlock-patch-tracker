Solomon remains calm and collected when facing a new challenger, so when the Patrons come calling, he approaches the city as if it's one giant chessboard. [i]Kingside Knight[/i] comes into play when it's time for defense, granting protection to allies and making tactical retreats when needed. [i]Rook Battery[/i] charges forward with great force, stalling enemies in their tracks.
